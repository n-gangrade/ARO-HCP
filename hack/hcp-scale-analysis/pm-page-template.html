Cores needed per region

Own cores are the clusters' share of the machines their control planes run on; amortized cores are their share of what's there for all the clusters, such as the machines that run logging, monitoring and cluster-management agents. Cores needed are the total rounded up to whole cores, each row on its own, so rows can add up to a little more than a total; best case is the same if management clusters are filled until they run out of room, keeping nothing spare. Share of a management cluster is how many full management clusters' worth the clusters take; if the region has no room, it needs that share rounded up to whole management clusters.
