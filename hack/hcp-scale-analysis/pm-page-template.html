Cores to request per region

Own cores are the clusters' share of the management cluster's machines their own control planes run on; amortized cores are their share of what's there for all the clusters, such as the machines that run logging, monitoring and cluster-management agents. Cores to request are rounded up to whole cores. Management clusters is how many full management clusters' worth the clusters take.
